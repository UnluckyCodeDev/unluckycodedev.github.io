About

I'm Gabriel Latchieu, a grade 12 student in Mississauga, Ontario with a current grade 12 average of 96%. I'm passionate about the intersection of software, hardware, and energy — specifically, how technology can make home electrification (heat pumps, retrofits, rebates) simpler and more accessible for contractors and homeowners.

My interest in engineering started with a simple question: how could a tool save a contractor time when sizing a heat pump or figuring out what rebates apply? That question became my first two projects, and it's the thread that connects everything I build.

Outside of coding, I serve as the lead altar server at Cristo Rei Parish in Mississauga, where I coordinate a team of 90 servers, organize the summer camp, and assist the priest during mass. I'm currently applying to engineering programs for Fall 2027 admission, with a focus on computer and electrical engineering.
